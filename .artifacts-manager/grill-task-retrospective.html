Grounding and proposed engineering defaults

Agent Fabric is currently a compiler/installer, not a session runtime. Its prompt-time hooks are distinct from native runtime hooks. Root-session visibility therefore needs explicit integration tests, not just an agent's primary-mode label.

Proposed defaults: configurable avoidable-action rate above 5%, a minimum sample and repetition/severity exceptions; typed Jev judgments with uncertainty; deterministic counting; task-scoped evidence with source versions; asynchronous dispatch with budgets and no self-trigger. Exact numeric defaults will be specified and tested in the plan rather than guessed by an implementer.
